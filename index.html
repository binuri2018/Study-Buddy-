Study Buddy STEM AI · Final Year Research · SLIIT · Project ID: R26-IT-009

An AI-Powered Personalized, Adaptive & Multimodal STEM Learning Ecosystem

Study Buddy STEM AI unifies four innovative AI modules—RAG-based personalized STEM storytelling, multilingual conversational tutoring, emotion-aware adaptive assessment, and handwritten note analysis with knowledge synthesis—grounded in the Sri Lankan GCE O/L curriculum for continuous, interactive learning.

Research focus: STEM education, generative AI, affective computing, and multimodal synthesis — built with semantic HTML, modern CSS, and minimal JavaScript.

Domain & scope

How we transform everyday STEM education: moving away from stressful memorization and one-size-fits-all classrooms to an intelligent, supportive study companion that understands how each student learns best.

01

Literature survey

Educational research shows that students thrive when lessons adapt to their unique pace, interests, and emotions. Rather than just generating answers, effective educational AI must act like a supportive personal mentor—guiding students step-by-step using officially approved school learning materials.

02

Research gap

Most existing study apps work in silos—one offers basic flashcards, another provides generic chatbots. Crucially, generic AI tools often invent incorrect facts, fail to follow local Sri Lankan syllabuses in Sinhala and English, ignore when students feel confused or anxious, and cannot understand handwritten student notes.

03

Problem statement

For many Sri Lankan O/L students, science feels abstract, dry, and intimidating. Heavy reliance on rote memorization for high-stakes exams leads to severe study stress, while crowded classrooms leave little opportunity for personalized attention or timely help when a student struggles with difficult concepts.

04

Objectives

Deliver Study Buddy STEM AI (Project ID: R26-IT-009) as a unified, student-centered learning companion that builds genuine understanding and confidence through four helpful pillars: relatable storytelling, natural voice & text conversations, stress-free adaptive quizzes, and smart revision from handwritten notes.

05

Methodology

Study Buddy connects official National Institute of Education (NIE) textbooks with four interconnected, student-friendly tools:

• Personalized Stories: Transforms abstract science theories into exciting, interactive adventures customized to each student's hobbies and future career interests.
• Friendly Multilingual Tutor: A patient voice and chat assistant that explains challenging topics with diagrams and clear examples in both Sinhala and English.
• Emotion-Aware Quizzes: Gently recognizes facial cues and hesitation patterns (like second-guessing or confusion) to adjust quiz difficulty, lower exam stress, and offer helpful hints when needed.
• Smart Note Synthesis: Allows students to snap photos of their handwritten notebooks, checks their notes against official textbooks for scientific accuracy, and automatically generates visual mind maps and revision flashcards.

06

Technologies

Structured across the four specialized AI components:

• Component 1 (Storytelling): Syllabus-grounded RAG, ChromaDB semantic vector search, and Random Forest machine learning for persona classification and interactive branching storylines.
• Component 2 (Conversational Tutor): High-accuracy conversational LLMs (Qwen2.5 7B & Mistral), OpenAI Whisper for bilingual voice questioning, and dynamic visual concept generators.
• Component 3 (Adaptive Assessment): Ultra-fast YOLOv8s computer vision (27.7 ms latency per frame) for real-time facial expression tracking, noise smoothing, and Random Forest cognitive-state prediction.
• Component 4 (Note Synthesis): Vision-language OCR (Gemma 4 & GPT-4o Vision) to scan handwritten notebooks, hybrid retrieval (FAISS + BM25 + BGE Reranker), and automated mind map/flashcard synthesis.
